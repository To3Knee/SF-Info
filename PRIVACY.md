# Privacy statement

**skullforge.net** · last updated 18 October 2026

Skullforge is a private homelab. It runs a few services for one household and a small team that works with it.
None of them is a public service: nobody outside can sign up, nothing is sold, and nothing is advertised. This
statement says what those services do with the information they can reach.

## What the services reach

Only what a person who uses them connects on purpose, for example:

- **Email and calendars** that the person signs in with, so a helper can read, sort and file mail, and read and add
  calendar events. Nothing is deleted for good, nothing is sent in the person's name, and no calendar entry is
  changed without that person saying so.
- **Documents** the person hands over to be filed.
- The **name and email address** a sign-in shares.

## Where it is kept

On private computers in the household, not on public servers. Sign-ins are stored encrypted on those computers. Notes
a helper keeps to do its job (what was asked, what was done) stay there too, and in backups the household controls.

## Who else processes it

Skullforge's helpers use frontier AI providers, such as **OpenAI** and **Anthropic**, to understand a request and
answer it. When a helper works on a request, the text it needs (the request, and any mail, calendar entry or note
needed to answer it) is sent to such a provider to be processed, and the answer comes back. Providers are used to do
the work only: nothing is sold, and nothing is used for advertising.

## What Skullforge never does

- Sell, rent or trade any information.
- Use any information for advertising.
- Share anyone's data except as described above, to do what that person asked.

## Google user data

Where a service connects to a Google account (Gmail, Google Calendar), it asks only after the person signs in with
Google and agrees on Google's own screen, and uses only the access that screen names. Its use and transfer to any
other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including
the Limited Use requirements. Access can be taken back at any time at
[myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions), which stops any further reading at once.

## Deleting it

Anyone can ask for everything Skullforge holds about them to be deleted, and it will be.

## Asking a question or making a request

[Open an issue in this repository](https://github.com/To3Knee/SF-Info/issues/new/choose) and choose **Privacy
request**. Issues are public, so **don't put personal details in it**: say what you'd like (a question, or a deletion)
and we'll reply there and take it from there.
