## What skullforge.net is

Skullforge is a homelab: a handful of machines in a family's home, and the services that run on them for that family
and a small team that works with it. It's built and looked after by hand, one piece at a time.

It is **not a public service.** There's nothing to sign up for, nothing for sale, and no advertising. If you've landed
here from a link, it's most likely because one of Skullforge's helpers asked you, or someone you know, to sign in with
an account, and the sign-in screen pointed here.

## What runs here

- **Personal helpers** that read and sort a person's mail, keep their calendar, file their paperwork and answer
  questions, each one for the people it was set up for, and only with what those people connect on purpose.
- **A small team's tools**: a meeting room, a chat room, and the mail relay the helpers use to write to the people
  they work with.

Everything stays on private machines in the household, with backups the household controls.

## Privacy, in one paragraph

Skullforge's helpers reach only what a person connects on purpose, keep it on private computers, and use frontier AI
providers (such as OpenAI and Anthropic) only to do the work a person asked for. Nothing is sold, rented or used for
advertising, and anyone can ask for what's held about them to be deleted. The full statement, including how Google
account data is handled, is in **[PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md)**.
